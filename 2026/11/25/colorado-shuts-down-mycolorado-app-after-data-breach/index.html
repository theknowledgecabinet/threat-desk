Colorado shuts down myColorado app after data breach

The state's digital ID and services app is offline after an unauthorised breach that may have exposed data for up to 150,000 users.

The Governor’s Office of Information Technology (OIT) confirmed that the myColorado app was taken offline following a cyberattack discovered on the evening of 18 November. The app, which provides digital identification, document storage, and access to state government services, has 2.1 million registered users.

According to OIT, an unauthorised user accessed data on the app’s platform, and the attack likely involved the theft of information tied to nearly 150,000 individuals. Investigators have not disclosed the specific types of data compromised. Sarah Tuneberg, executive director of OIT, stated that protecting user data is the priority and systems will be restored once security is confirmed.

Colorado has urged residents to carry physical identification for travel during the Thanksgiving holiday. State services remain accessible via individual agency websites while the app remains unavailable.

Affected

myColorado app

Actions

  • Monitor for official notifications regarding specific data elements exposed.
  • Use physical identification documents for travel and verification until the app is restored.
  • Access state services through individual agency websites as an alternative to the app.
