ThreatsDay: 10M NGINX Services at Risk, 250+ Malicious Extensions

A new ThreatsDay report highlights over 10 million NGINX services exposed to risk and more than 250 malicious browser extensions, alongside AI server hijacking incidents.

According to The Hacker News, the latest ThreatsDay report identifies 10 million NGINX services at risk. The report also details the discovery of over 250 malicious browser extensions.

The article notes that browser extensions can behave differently depending on the user context, such as whether a user is logged in or out. It highlights that stolen logins are being used to exploit these discrepancies, allowing malicious software to function in ways that are not immediately visible to the site operator.

The report further covers incidents involving AI server hijacking and 17 additional stories, though specific technical details for these individual incidents are not provided in the source text.

Affected

NGINX services, browser extensions

Actions

  • Audit browser extensions for unexpected behaviour in different user states
  • Review NGINX configurations to reduce exposure of services
  • Monitor for signs of AI server hijacking
