Ex-OpenAI employee granted $1m bail in bugging case

A former OpenAI employee has been granted bail after being accused of installing covert recording devices in the company's San Francisco offices.

A San Francisco federal court judge ruled on Tuesday that Abdulrahman Alfozan, a 35-year-old Saudi national, may be released from jail on a $1 million bond. Alfozan is a former OpenAI employee who was arrested on 9 October 2026.

He is accused of concealing a covert recording device at the artificial intelligence company's offices. The charges allege the bugging was carried out with a specific aim, though the full details of the intent were not specified in the report.

The case highlights physical security risks for technology firms, where insider threats can involve the installation of hardware surveillance equipment within secure facilities.

Actions

  • Conduct regular physical security audits of office spaces to detect unauthorised hardware.
  • Implement strict access control and monitoring for former employees.
  • Review incident response plans for physical intrusion and data exfiltration via hardware.
