AFP takes content platform offline after cyberattack

Agence France-Presse has taken its AFP Forum platform offline following a confirmed cyberattack, disrupting news distribution for global media clients.

Agence France-Presse (AFP) confirmed on Thursday that it was hit by a cyberattack targeting AFP Forum, the platform used by news outlets to browse and download the agency’s stories, photographs, and videos. In an internal email quoted in its own reporting, AFP management stated that the intrusion was detected overnight and that the platform was taken offline immediately as a precautionary measure.

The agency is currently investigating the circumstances of the incident, including the identity of the perpetrators, with assistance from external cybersecurity experts. AFP has not identified who was behind the attack or provided a timeline for restoring the service. It remains unclear whether the attackers gained access to sensitive information or if other AFP systems were affected.

AFP Forum remained inaccessible as of Friday, displaying a maintenance notice in several languages. The agency is offering subscribers alternative ways to access its content while the platform remains offline. This is not the first cyberattack to disrupt the agency’s services; in September 2024, AFP detected an attack on its IT systems that disrupted some content delivery services, which has not been publicly attributed to any hacking group.
