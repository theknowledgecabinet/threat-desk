Korean engineering association breach leaks 210,000 member records

The Korea Scientists and Engineers Mutual-aid Association confirmed a cyber breach that exposed sensitive member data, including internal identification codes.

The Korea Scientists and Engineers Mutual-aid Association detected a cyber breach early this month. According to industry sources, the organisation commissioned an outside specialist agency to conduct a detailed investigation.

The probe confirmed on 25 November that approximately 210,000 pieces of member information had been leaked. The compromised data includes membership numbers and 13 types of internal identification codes.

The incident highlights the risk to professional bodies holding sensitive personal data, with the association now managing the fallout from the exposure.

Actions

  • Monitor for phishing attempts targeting members using leaked identification codes.
  • Review access logs for unauthorised activity in similar professional databases.
  • Ensure sensitive internal codes are encrypted at rest and in transit.
