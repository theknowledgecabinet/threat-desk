FIFA confirms cyberattack exposed personal and business data

FIFA has confirmed it was the victim of a cyberattack that resulted in the unauthorised access to a limited set of personal and business information.

FIFA stated on Thursday that it is working closely with relevant authorities, including law enforcement, following the incident. Reportedly, the organisation did not specify when the cyberattack occurred or the precise nature of the compromised data.

The breach follows a claim by a group calling itself the Handsome Boy Hacking Club, which posted a screenshot on X last week of what it said were FIFA files. Three days later, the group appeared to take credit for the hack in response to a post alleging that more than 12 gigabytes of internal documents had been leaked.

FIFA condemned the unlawful acquisition and publication of information, describing it as a serious violation of privacy. The statement noted that such actions can have significant consequences for the individuals concerned.
