Flight Centre investigating third-party breach that may have exposed customer records

A third-party accommodation booking system breach may have exposed customer records, including a very small number containing passport details, for Flight Centre and its leisure brands.

Flight Centre Travel Group disclosed on 26 November 2026 that it is investigating a cyber incident involving a third-party accommodation booking system. The company detected unusual activity in a platform used by its leisure travel brands, including Flight Centre, Travel Associates, and Cruiseabout, as well as some independent agents. Flight bookings and corporate travel bookings, which rely on separate systems, were not affected.

According to Reuters, the company has not yet determined the volume of records accessed. Initial reviews indicate that most accessed records contained customer names, while some included basic travel details. Flight Centre believes a very small number of records contained passport details, email addresses, phone numbers, and dates of birth. In rare cases, records may have included health or mobility information. The company stated it found no evidence that credit card, payment, or bank account details were accessed.

Flight Centre has activated its incident response plan and is investigating with the supplier and cybersecurity firm Mandiant. The company is working to identify affected systems, contain the incident, change authentication keys and passwords, and increase monitoring. It is also notifying relevant authorities and working with external security experts.

Affected

Third-party accommodation booking system used by Flight Centre, Travel Associates, Cruiseabout, and some independent agents

Actions

  • Monitor for phishing attempts using exposed travel details, such as hotel names or booking references.
  • Avoid clicking links or opening attachments in unsolicited messages regarding travel bookings.
  • Do not share personal information, passwords, or payment details in response to unsolicited requests.
  • Wait for official communication from Flight Centre regarding specific data exposure.
