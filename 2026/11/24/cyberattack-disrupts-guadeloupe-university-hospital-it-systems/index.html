Cyberattack disrupts Guadeloupe university hospital IT systems

A major cyberattack on Saturday night forced the Guadeloupe university hospital to activate its emergency response plan and postpone non-emergency procedures.

According to RFI, a significant cyberattack disrupted the information and telephone systems of the university hospital in the French overseas department of Guadeloupe. The incident occurred on Saturday night and left staff unable to access several applications and databases.

The disruption forced the hospital to activate its emergency response plan and postpone non-emergency procedures.

Actions

  • Ensure emergency response plans are tested and ready for activation during IT outages.
  • Verify that critical clinical applications have offline or manual fallback procedures.
  • Monitor for unusual activity in hospital information systems and telephone networks.
