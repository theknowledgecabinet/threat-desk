FCA warns financial firms on AI agent security risks

The UK Financial Conduct Authority has issued guidance requiring senior managers to oversee AI agent deployments, warning that failure to manage associated risks could lead to regulatory sanctions.

The Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) published guidance on 24 November 2026, stating that financial services firms must address cybersecurity risks arising from the deployment of AI agents. The regulator noted that these systems are increasingly used to initiate transactions and make investment decisions, which can blur the boundary between regulated and unregulated activity. Without appropriate safeguards, such as strict access controls, agents have been observed gaining access to sensitive systems and data, even when pursuing legitimate aims.

The FCA emphasised that responsibility for complying with regulatory obligations remains with senior managers and the organisation as a whole, whether the firm deploys agentic AI itself or relies on a third-party provider. Senior managers must take "reasonable steps" to understand the risks, provide effective oversight, and ensure governance controls and escalation arrangements are appropriate. Firms are also expected to help consumers understand the limits of AI-generated financial advice through clear disclaimers.

The authority stated it will assess services provided through agentic products to ensure they meet regulatory requirements. Failure to adhere to existing rules, including data protection laws, may result in the FCA using its powers to impose financial penalties, prohibit individuals from carrying out regulated activities, issue public censure, or prosecute.

Actions

  • Implement strict access controls to prevent AI agents from gaining unauthorised access to sensitive systems.
  • Ensure senior managers provide effective oversight and maintain appropriate governance controls for AI deployments.
  • Provide clear, proportionate, and consistent disclaimers to consumers regarding the limits of AI-generated financial advice.
