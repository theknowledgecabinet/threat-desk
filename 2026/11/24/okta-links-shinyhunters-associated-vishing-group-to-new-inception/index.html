Okta links ShinyHunters-associated vishing group to new INCEPTION data leak site

Okta Threat Intelligence assesses that a ShinyHunters-associated cluster may have rebranded as INCEPTION, continuing vishing attacks to harvest credentials and approvals.

Okta’s Threat Intelligence team has assessed that a threat cluster, designated O-UNC-053, previously affiliated with the ShinyHunters extortion crew, may have rebranded as INCEPTION. The group is characterised by the use of voice phishing (vishing) attacks to deceive targets into entering credentials on phishing pages and approving malicious actions.

According to Okta, this activity is linked to a new data leak site operating under the INCEPTION name. The assessment suggests the cluster has continued its operations under the new identity rather than ceasing activity.

Actions

  • Warn users about vishing attempts requesting credential entry or approval.
  • Monitor for anomalous authentication events and approval requests.
  • Review Okta Threat Intelligence reports for indicators of compromise.
