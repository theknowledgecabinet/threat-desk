Iran-aligned Volant Manticore uses IPFS-based KuboShell backdoor

A previously undocumented Iran-aligned cyber espionage group has targeted telecommunications, airlines, and critical infrastructure in Cyprus using a new IPFS-based backdoor.

The Hacker News reports that a group identified as Volant Manticore, described as Iran-aligned, has conducted espionage campaigns against telecommunications companies, airlines, and critical infrastructure in Cyprus. The group’s operations rely on a backdoor named KuboShell, which utilises the InterPlanetary File System (IPFS) for command-and-control (C2) communications.

According to the report, KuboShell deploys Homa, a post-compromise framework described as a reimplementation of an existing tool. The article notes that the group and its tooling were previously undocumented, highlighting a new threat vector for defenders monitoring C2 traffic.

Actions

  • Monitor network traffic for unusual IPFS-related activity or connections to known KuboShell infrastructure.
  • Review endpoint logs for signs of the Homa post-compromise framework.
  • Assess exposure of telecommunications, aviation, and critical infrastructure assets in Cyprus to potential espionage targeting.
