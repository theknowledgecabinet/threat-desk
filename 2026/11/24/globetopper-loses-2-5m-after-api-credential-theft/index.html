GlobeTopper loses $2.5m after API credential theft

A cyberattack on B2B gift card distributor GlobeTopper resulted in $2.5m in lost revenue and the redemption of $70,000 in digital gift cards.

Parent company iQSTEL disclosed in a US Securities and Exchange Commission filing that it detected unauthorised activity at its subsidiary GlobeTopper on 22 October 2026. The company suspended its platform and took affected servers offline on 23 October to investigate the intrusion. A rebuilt platform was deployed on AWS on 31 October, with commercial operations restored in stages from 1 November.

According to iQSTEL, the attacker obtained API credentials and integration keys belonging to certain business customers. The company stated that all affected credentials have been revoked and rotated, and new credentials have been issued to affected customers with additional access restrictions. An investigation is ongoing to determine whether other customer, personal, payment, or provider data was accessed.

The incident caused an estimated $2.5 million in lost revenue, while the attacker redeemed digital gift cards worth $70,000. GlobeTopper stated it cannot block, recover, or reclaim the redeemed cards and will incur the losses. No ransomware groups have listed iQSTEL or GlobeTopper as victims on their extortion portals.

Affected

GlobeTopper platform, client API credentials, integration keys

Actions

  • Audit API credentials and integration keys for unauthorised access.
  • Rotate and revoke any potentially compromised credentials immediately.
  • Implement additional access restrictions for sensitive API endpoints.
  • Monitor for unauthorised redemption of digital assets or gift cards.
