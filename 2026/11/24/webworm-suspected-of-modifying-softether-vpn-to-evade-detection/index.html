Webworm suspected of modifying SoftEther VPN to evade detection

A China-aligned threat actor is suspected of using a customised SoftEther VPN Bridge to hide remote access and bypass security tools.

The Hacker News reports that the China-aligned threat actor known as Webworm is suspected of deploying a custom version of the open-source SoftEther VPN Bridge tool. The group is suspected of modifying the software to remove recognizable detection fingerprints and disguise it as network-monitoring software, thereby establishing covert remote access to compromised networks.

According to the report, the modified variant "implements various tricks to hide itself from security tools." This activity highlights the risk of open-source utilities being repurposed by state-aligned actors to maintain persistent access while evading standard defensive measures.

Affected

SoftEther VPN Bridge

Actions

  • Audit network traffic for unauthorised instances of SoftEther VPN Bridge.
  • Review software inventories for unexpected network-monitoring tools.
  • Monitor for known Webworm tradecraft and detection evasion techniques.
