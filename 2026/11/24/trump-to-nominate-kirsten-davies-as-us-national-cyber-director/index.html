Trump to nominate Kirsten Davies as US National Cyber Director

The White House intends to appoint the Pentagon’s chief information officer to the role permanently following strong industry support.

President Donald Trump intends to nominate Kirsten Davies, the Department of Defense’s chief information officer, as the next National Cyber Director, according to a White House official. Davies was named acting director last week after the departure of Sean Cairncross. The decision follows what the official described as “overwhelming” support from industry and government leaders.

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton will serve in the role while Davies awaits Senate confirmation. Clayton stated that Davies has the “experience and capabilities needed to help America navigate our increasingly complex and powerful digital infrastructure.”

Davies previously served as chief information security officer at Unilever and Estée Lauder, and held security leadership roles at Barclays Africa, Hewlett Packard, Siemens, Booz Allen Hamilton, and Deloitte Australia. The appointment comes amid reports that the FBI’s National Crime Information Center has been hacked, following a major cyberattack in September that exposed personal information of FBI agents and Justice Department staffers.
