AI-driven campaign compromises 24 Check Point management servers

GreyNoise has identified a suspected AI-assisted intrusion campaign targeting Check Point Security Management servers across 22 organisations in the financial, energy, and government sectors.

An unknown threat actor is using artificial intelligence-driven offensive tooling in a campaign tracked by GreyNoise as GreyNoise Threat 13 (GT13). The activity has resulted in the compromise of 24 Check Point Security Management servers, impacting 22 distinct organisations globally.

According to The Hacker News, the intrusion activity is assessed to have targeted organisations in the financial services, energy, and government sectors. The report notes the use of AI-driven tooling in the offensive operations, though specific technical details regarding the exploitation method are not provided in the source text.

GreyNoise is monitoring the activity under the GT13 moniker. The campaign represents a significant risk to security infrastructure, as the compromise of management servers can potentially allow attackers to bypass perimeter defences and access internal networks.

Affected

Check Point Security Management servers

Actions

  • Review logs for anomalous activity on Check Point Security Management servers.
  • Monitor for indicators associated with GreyNoise Threat 13 (GT13).
  • Ensure management interfaces are not exposed to the public internet.
  • Verify the integrity of management server configurations and credentials.
