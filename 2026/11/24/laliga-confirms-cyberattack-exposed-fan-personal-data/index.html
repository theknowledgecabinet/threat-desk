LaLiga confirms cyberattack exposed fan personal data

Spain's top football league disclosed that a cybercriminal accessed its fan services platform between 14 and 16 November, extracting personal data from registered users.

LaLiga, the organiser of Spain's top two men's professional football divisions, confirmed a data breach affecting users of its digital services and those of some affiliated clubs. The organisation stated that a cybercriminal gained unauthorised access to one of its technology platforms for fan services between 14 and 16 November 2026.

According to LaLiga, its security team identified the source of the unauthorised access on 16 November and implemented measures to block the attack and stop the data extraction. The incident was confined to the specific fan services platform and did not extend to other systems supporting the running of the competitions.

LaLiga reported the incident to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) and filed a complaint with the National Police’s Central Cybercrime Unit. Impacted clubs were directly notified, and actions to inform affected fans are being coordinated. No ransomware group has claimed responsibility for the attack as of the time of writing.

Affected

LaLiga technology platform for fan services

Actions

  • Monitor for phishing attempts targeting fans using stolen personal data.
  • Review access logs for unauthorised activity on fan-facing platforms.
  • Coordinate with law enforcement and data protection authorities for incident response.
