Signal blocks OS AI agents from reading chats

Signal has implemented platform-specific measures to prevent iOS and Android AI agents from accessing user messages, citing a lack of control over data collection by operating system features.

Meredith Whittaker, president of the Signal Foundation, described AI agents integrated into operating systems, such as Google Gemini on Android and Apple's Siri AI, as "an existential threat to Signal" in a 20 November blog post. The organisation stated it could not control what these agents collected, prompting the deployment of new protections to stop them from capturing user chats.

On Android, Signal said the existing 'Screen security' setting would switch on automatically when an AI assistant attempts to read the screen. The company explained that because multiple Android versions exist, there is no reliable way to detect screenshot attempts if screenshots are already disabled, so it chose detection over blocking all screenshots to preserve user experience. On iOS 27, Signal is preventing screenshots and displaying a 'Block Siri AI' setting. However, on macOS 27, Signal said it had no dependable way to stop screenshots, as Apple's screen-protection options cover only streaming and copy-protected media.

Signal also noted that third-party agents, such as Meta Muse, use accessibility settings to reach app data. The company has asked Apple, Google, and Microsoft for a documented way for apps to opt out of AI access to screens, files, and notifications without breaking functionality. Whittaker said Signal's developers had conducted forensic analysis of Android and iOS to understand how AI systems reach app data, noting that the lack of documentation makes strong privacy harder to build.

Affected

iOS 27, Android, macOS 27, Windows 11

Actions

  • Enable 'Screen security' on Android to allow automatic detection of AI screen-reading attempts.
  • Use the 'Block Siri AI' setting on iOS 27 to prevent agent access.
  • Add Signal to the Siri Excluded Apps list on macOS 27, as native screenshot blocking is unavailable.
  • Review accessibility permissions for third-party apps that may use AI agents to access data.
