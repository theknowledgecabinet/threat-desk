FIFA confirms cyberattack exposed limited personal and business data

FIFA has confirmed that hackers accessed internal systems, following a claim by the Handsome Boy Hacking Club that it stole over 12 GB of data.

The Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA) stated on 26 November that it was the victim of a cyberattack resulting in the "unauthorised access to a limited set of personal and business information." The organisation said it is working closely with relevant authorities, including law enforcement, and condemned the "unlawful acquisition and publication" of the data.

The Handsome Boy Hacking Club (HBHC) claimed last week that it stole more than 12 GB of data from FIFA and attempted to extort the organisation. The group said it accessed FIFA’s Microsoft environment and published the stolen data on a cybercriminal forum. The data allegedly includes identity and immigration documents, financial records, legal documents, and governance information.

FIFA did not respond to follow-up questions regarding the specific nature of the incident. The breach follows recent cyberattacks on other European soccer entities, including LaLiga and the French Football Federation.

Actions

  • Monitor for unauthorised access to internal systems and data exfiltration.
  • Review and restrict access to sensitive personal and business information.
  • Coordinate with law enforcement and relevant authorities in the event of a breach.
