CIA OSINT system reportedly breached; FinCEN takes systems offline

Unknown attackers reportedly used the ARTEX AI framework to breach an unclassified CIA system, while FinCEN disclosed a separate incident prompting precautionary system shutdowns.

According to a source familiar with the matter, unknown attackers reportedly breached an open-source intelligence (OSINT) analysis system at the US Central Intelligence Agency (CIA). The source told The New York Times that the cyberattack was carried out using the ARTEX AI-powered penetration testing framework and also affected the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) and the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency. The CIA and ODNI have not yet replied to requests for details, but the source stated that the hack did not involve classified networks or information, and no other systems are currently at risk.

In a related development, the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) disclosed it is investigating a cybersecurity incident involving unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems. After detecting the attack, the agency took some systems offline as a precaution and instructed banks to use the Financial Institutions Hotline to file reports. A FinCEN official stated that Treasury is working with the FBI, CISA, and third-party forensic examiners to determine the scope and impact of the incident. While forensic analysis remains ongoing, there is no evidence of any breach of beneficial ownership databases or the Bank Secrecy Act.

These incidents follow a series of other cyberattacks on US federal agencies in recent years, including a breach of the FBI's National Crime Information Center (NCIC) reported over the weekend. Additionally, the US National Security Agency's classified network suffered a days-long outage one week ago, which the agency attributed to scheduled routine maintenance.

Affected

CIA OSINT analysis system; FinCEN systems

Actions

  • Monitor for unusual activity in OSINT and unclassified systems.
  • Verify the integrity of financial reporting systems if connected to FinCEN services.
  • Review access logs for AI-powered penetration testing frameworks like ARTEX.
