Michigan nurse jailed for $1.6m Medicare fraud using stolen records

A home health care owner was sentenced to four years in prison for billing Medicare for services that were never performed, using stolen patient data.

Ruby Scott, a nurse from Farmington Hills, Michigan, was sentenced to four years in federal prison on Thursday. She owned and operated Delta Home Health Care LLC.

According to a press release from the Justice Department, Scott used stolen patient records to bill Medicare for fake services. The total value of the fraud scheme was reported as $1.6 million.

Actions

  • Audit billing records for discrepancies between service delivery and claims.
  • Implement strict access controls for patient health information used in billing.
  • Monitor for unauthorised use of patient data in financial transactions.
