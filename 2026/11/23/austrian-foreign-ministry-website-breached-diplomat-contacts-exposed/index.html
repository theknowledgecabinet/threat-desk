Austrian Foreign Ministry website breached, diplomat contacts exposed

A breach of an external website exposed official contact details for Austrian diplomats, with no attribution yet made.

Austria's Foreign Ministry confirmed that hackers breached one of its websites between 12 and 13 November, accessing the names and official contact information of employees at its Vienna headquarters and diplomatic missions abroad. The incident affected a website operated by an external service provider, and the ministry stated that the breach has been contained.

The ministry said no personal information belonging to members of the public was compromised. It has notified Austria's cybersecurity and data protection authorities but has not disclosed specific technical vulnerabilities or security measures for security reasons. The attack coincided with a global Microsoft service outage, though Austrian authorities have not established a link between the two incidents.

There is no public evidence linking Russia to this latest cyberattack, despite the ministry having been targeted by suspected state-sponsored hackers linked to Moscow in 2019 and 2020. Vienna publicly attributed that earlier attack to Russia in July 2026, amid worsening diplomatic relations following the expulsion of Russian diplomats and a drone strike on the Austrian Embassy in Kyiv.

Affected

Austrian Foreign Ministry website (operated by external service provider)

Actions

  • Verify the integrity of external service provider websites and access controls.
  • Monitor for unauthorised access to diplomatic or sensitive contact databases.
  • Review incident response procedures for third-party managed infrastructure.
