Peninsula Energy warns of disruption after Wyoming cyber incident

An Australian-listed energy company has requested a trading halt to investigate a cybersecurity breach at its uranium project in Wyoming.

Peninsula Energy stated that a cybersecurity incident at its Lance uranium project in Wyoming would temporarily affect operations. The company is currently investigating a breach of its operating system.

The incident occurred on Friday, according to the company. Peninsula Energy, which is listed in Australia, requested a trading halt to prepare an announcement regarding the event.

A filing dated Monday described the situation as preliminary while the investigation continues.
