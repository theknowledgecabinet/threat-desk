Careers360 breach exposes 22M records including exam results

A dataset of 22 million records from the Indian education platform Careers360 appeared on a public forum, containing sensitive academic and personal data.

In November 2026, data allegedly taken from the Indian education and careers platform Careers360 appeared on a public hacking forum. According to Have I Been Pwned, the dataset comprises 22 million records, including 3.6 million unique email addresses.

The compromised data includes names, phone numbers, genders, and caste categories. Most records also contain students' entrance exam results, specifically ranks and marks. The breach was added to the Have I Been Pwned database on 20 November 2026, with attribution to 'Threat Actor 888'.

No specific vendor advisory or confirmed exploitation status beyond the data's appearance on the forum is provided in the source.

Affected

Careers360

Actions

  • Change passwords for any accounts where the affected Careers360 password was reused.
  • Enable two-factor authentication on all supported accounts.
