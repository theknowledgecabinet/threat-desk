Too Lost breach exposes 724k unique email addresses

An unauthorised party claims to have obtained data from music distributor Too Lost, exposing sensitive personal and financial details for 724k unique email addresses.

In November 2026, an unauthorised party claimed to have obtained data from Too Lost, a music distributor for independent artists. According to Have I Been Pwned, the compromised dataset contains 724k unique email addresses belonging to artists, their collaborators and fans.

The data reportedly includes bcrypt password hashes, partial credit card data (last 4 digits and card type), and identity verification information (in some cases, passport, driver's licence or national ID numbers). Additional fields include dates of birth, physical addresses, IP addresses, and payment histories.

Too Lost stated that its investigation remains ongoing and that external forensic specialists have been engaged. The company said measures have been taken to secure affected systems and credentials, and it is working with advisors to coordinate communications and support for affected individuals.

Affected

Too Lost

Actions

  • Change passwords immediately on every account where the affected password was used.
  • Enable two-factor authentication wherever supported.
