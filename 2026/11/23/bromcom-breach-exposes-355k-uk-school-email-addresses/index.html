Bromcom breach exposes 355k UK school email addresses

A data breach at UK education software provider Bromcom exposed 355k email addresses and SSO reference numbers, though the company states no passwords or system access were compromised.

In September 2026, UK education software provider Bromcom suffered a data breach involving a legacy single sign-on (SSO) registration system. The incident exposed 355k unique email addresses belonging to staff and pupils at UK schools, alongside SSO registration reference numbers.

Bromcom confirmed that no passwords or authentication tokens were exposed. The company stated that the incident did not enable access to Microsoft or Google accounts or its school management system. The breach was added to the Have I Been Pwned database on 23 November 2026.

Affected

Bromcom legacy single sign-on (SSO) registration system

Actions

  • Change passwords for any accounts where the exposed email addresses are used, if not already done.
  • Enable two-factor authentication wherever supported to add an extra layer of security.
