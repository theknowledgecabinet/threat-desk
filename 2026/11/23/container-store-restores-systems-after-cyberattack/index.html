Container Store restores systems after cyberattack

Neighborhood Intelligence confirmed The Container Store has restored its website and systems following a weekend incident that disrupted online ordering and in-store transactions.

Neighborhood Intelligence, the parent company of The Container Store, stated that the retailer has restored its website and systems following a cybersecurity incident that occurred over the weekend. The disruption forced the company to shut down online ordering systems and implement alternative transaction-processing procedures in its approximately 100 US stores, although physical locations remained open.

The company said it engaged cybersecurity experts to contain the incident and restore affected systems. While it reported that the incident did not affect other brands under the Neighborhood Intelligence umbrella, such as Bed Bath & Beyond or Overstock, it did not address claims made by the cybercriminal group n0n, which listed the company on its leak site earlier this month.

According to cybersecurity firm LevelBlue, the n0n group has primarily targeted healthcare and pharmaceuticals but recently claimed to have data from financial services and retail firms. LevelBlue noted that the group uses AI coding agents and paid AI services to write and debug attack scripts, helping it scale operations quickly.

Actions

  • Monitor for unusual activity in retail and e-commerce systems, particularly during peak trading periods.
  • Review third-party vendor security practices, especially those using automated or AI-assisted development tools.
  • Ensure incident response plans include procedures for alternative transaction processing to maintain business continuity.
