KEPCO confirms breach exposing 1.5 million individuals

South Korea's main power utility confirmed a breach of its billing and procurement systems, exposing sensitive data for over 1.5 million individuals.

Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO) confirmed on 20 November 2026 that a threat actor accessed its internal network, specifically targeting the KEPCO ON electricity billing platform and the electronic procurement system (SRM). The company stated it detected the unauthorised access on that date and subsequently blocked external IP addresses and suspended the affected services to contain the incident.

According to KEPCO, the breach exposed personal and financial details for 650,487 customers, including names, ID information, bank account numbers, and card details. Additionally, data for 426,827 company representatives and 442,934 employees was compromised, with the latter group's exposure including employee ID numbers and contact information. The company attributed the breach to "advanced technologies such as AI" but provided no specific details on the initial access vector or attack chain.

A threat actor using the alias "Noiem" claimed responsibility on a hacker forum on 19 November, advertising the theft of user data, supplier records, and bidding documents. While South Korea’s Personal Information Protection Commission confirmed KEPCO received a theft claim email on that date, attribution to Noiem remains unconfirmed. KEPCO is notifying affected individuals and coordinating with government and financial authorities to mitigate potential abuse.

Affected

KEPCO ON electricity billing platform; electronic procurement system (SRM)

Actions

  • Monitor for targeted phishing or impersonation attempts using exposed personal and financial data.
  • Report any suspicious activity or unauthorised transactions to the police or Korea’s Internet and Security Agency.
  • Avoid installing unknown applications or entering information into suspicious forms.
  • Do not share sensitive data or verification codes over calls or texts.
