Kirsten Davies appointed US National Cyber Director

The Department of War CIO replaces Sean Cairncross as the new National Cyber Director.

Kirsten Davies, currently serving as Chief Information Officer at the Department of War, has been appointed as the new National Cyber Director. She replaces Sean Cairncross, who was confirmed for the position in August 2025. Davies previously held senior security roles at Unilever, The Estée Lauder Companies Inc., Hewlett-Packard, Siemens, and Barclays.

The appointment follows a series of significant outages across the US, including disruptions to Microsoft 365, the Federal Aviation Administration, and JPMorgan Chase. The Trump Administration has stated there is “no indication of a coordinated cyberattack, state-sponsored activity, or malign actor behind the recent tech outages.”

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton and Secretary of State Marco Rubio explained that the incidents were unrelated and stemmed from companies patching vulnerabilities and upgrading outdated systems. This context coincides with a reported surge in AI-assisted cyberattacks, primarily powered by the Chinese agentic AI penetration testing framework Artex.

  • Review patching and system upgrade procedures to prevent communication failures.
