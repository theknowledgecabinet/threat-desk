SBC Medical Group breach exposes data of 2m Japanese individuals

A data breach affecting over two million individuals in Japan has been disclosed by SBC Medical Group, with attackers accessing basic personal information, employee credentials, and credit card details.

SBC Medical Group Holdings Incorporated, a California-based medical services organisation, disclosed in a filing with the US Securities and Exchange Commission that hackers accessed an online platform it manages. The company stated that databases containing customer information for certain clinics in Japan were likely compromised, impacting over 2 million individuals.

According to the filing, the attackers stole basic personal information for 1,624,000 individuals, including names, addresses, dates of birth, and patient card numbers. Additionally, reservation-request information for 255,416 people and web inquiry form content for 217,769 individuals were exfiltrated. The breach also affected 15,007 individuals whose treatment names and review text were stolen, and 14 individuals whose credit card information was compromised, although SBC stated these cards were expired or otherwise unable to process additional payments.

Employee data was also targeted, with 20,000 records containing names, IDs, and passwords stolen. For 459 employees, sensitive information provided at the time of employment, including bank account details and withholding tax certificates, was affected. In a separate incident involving the third-party reservation service Tribeau, administrators accounts were accessed, compromising the information of approximately 19,000 individuals, though the operator confirmed that electronic medical records and photographs were not included in that specific platform incident.

SBC Medical Group stated that both incidents have been remediated. The company isolated the affected server, evicted the attackers, and rotated passwords for all clinics it manages on Tribeau. It is continuing to assess the potential impact on its business and financial condition.

Affected

SBC Medical Group online platform; Tribeau reservation service

Actions

  • Rotate credentials for any systems where passwords may have been exposed.
  • Monitor for fraudulent activity on credit cards and financial accounts associated with affected individuals.
  • Verify the integrity of third-party service provider accounts and access logs.
  • Isolate potentially compromised servers to prevent further data exfiltration.
