Cleverbridge breach exposes payment card numbers and CVVs

E-commerce platform Cleverbridge confirmed that attackers stole customer payment card data, including CVV codes, after detecting unauthorised activity in early November.

Cleverbridge, which provides e-commerce services to software and SaaS companies, stated that it detected unauthorised activity on 4 November 2026. The company temporarily took systems offline to contain the incident and brought them back online the same day. Subsequent investigation determined that a third party copied payment card data stored on the platform.

According to a breach notification shared with BleepingComputer, the stolen information includes payment card numbers, expiration dates, and CVV/CVC security codes. Cleverbridge stated that no other personal data, such as names or billing details, were copied. The company confirmed the incident was not a ransomware attack and that no systems were encrypted.

Cleverbridge began notifying affected customers on 20 November 2026. It has informed data protection authorities, law enforcement, and payment card networks of the breach. The company is working with TransUnion's Cyberscout to provide complimentary credit monitoring and fraud assistance to affected individuals.

Affected

Cleverbridge e-commerce platform

Actions

  • Review payment card statements for unauthorised transactions.
  • Contact card issuers regarding the incident, as banks may replace compromised cards.
