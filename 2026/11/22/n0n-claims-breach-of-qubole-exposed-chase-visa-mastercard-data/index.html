n0n claims breach of Qubole exposed Chase, Visa, Mastercard data

The n0n ransomware group alleges it stole marketing and customer data from cloud analytics firm Qubole, affecting J.P. Morgan Chase, Visa, and Mastercard.

J.P. Morgan Chase stated that a cyberattack on a third-party service provider exposed certain marketing data, which may include customer names and email addresses. The bank confirmed the incident did not disrupt its services and is unrelated to a separate service disruption earlier in the week. Visa and Mastercard separately confirmed third-party incidents, with Visa noting its systems were not involved and no payment data was affected. Mastercard said the exposure involved a limited amount of email addresses from a small subset of cardholders enrolled in a specific benefit program.

The n0n ransomware group claimed responsibility for the attack on Qubole, a cloud data analytics company owned by Idera, on 11 November 2026. According to the group, attackers used compromised Qubole credentials between 14 and 26 October 2026 to access customers' AWS environments via IAM roles. n0n alleges it stole 53.9 GB of data directly from Qubole and nearly one petabyte from customer networks, including contracts, pricing records, and production data.

n0n published the claimed data on its extortion site on 16 November 2026. The group stated it contacted Qubole and affected companies before public reports from Sentra and Wiz detailed the attacks, but its extortion demands were ignored. BleepingComputer contacted Qubole for comment but had not received a response as of publication.

Affected

Qubole cloud data analytics platform; J.P. Morgan Chase, Visa, and Mastercard customer data

Actions

  • Review IAM roles and third-party access permissions to cloud environments.
  • Monitor for unauthorised access to AWS environments via service provider credentials.
  • Verify the integrity of data held by third-party analytics providers.
  • Assess exposure of marketing and customer contact data in third-party systems.
