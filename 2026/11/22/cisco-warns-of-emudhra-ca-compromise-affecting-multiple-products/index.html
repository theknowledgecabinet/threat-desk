Cisco warns of eMudhra CA compromise affecting multiple products

Cisco has issued an emergency advisory after a critical compromise of eMudhra certificate authorities rendered eight intermediate certificates vulnerable to brute-force key recovery.

Between 17 and 20 October 2026, a critical security compromise involving Certificate Authorities managed by eMudhra was uncovered. A security researcher demonstrated that the private keys for eight eMudhra intermediate certificates were fundamentally broken due to an error in their Key Generation Ceremony in April 2025, allowing anyone to brute-force acquire these private keys within seconds.

Cisco has investigated its product line and identified numerous impacted devices, including IOS XE, Secure Firewall, and Identity Services Engine. For products where software fixes are available, Cisco has provided target release dates ranging from November 2026 to March 2027. Some cloud-based products, such as Duo Cloud and Meraki Dashboard, have been remediated without requiring customer action.

For products with immediate remediation steps, Cisco advises administrators to apply specific distrust guides or update trusted root lists. For example, Secure Email and Web Manager users should update to Trusted Root 2.8, while IOS XE users should follow the Cisco IOS XE and GuestShell CA Trust eMudhra Distrust Guide.
