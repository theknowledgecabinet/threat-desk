Latvian telecom leak exposes 125m SMS records

The Latvian branch of CSC Telecom left a database containing over 125 million SMS messages exposed, affecting clients of Swedbank, SEB, airBaltic, and dozens of other companies.

According to DataBreaches.net, the Latvian branch of CSC Telecom left a database containing over 125 million SMS messages exposed. The incident was reported on 11 November by Vilius Petkauskas and categorised under the site’s “No need to hack when it’s leaking” files.

The leaked records span from October 2022 to September 2026. The data breach exposes clients of Swedbank, SEB, airBaltic, and dozens of other companies.

Affected

CSC Telecom Latvian branch SMS database

Actions

  • Audit external-facing storage for unauthorised access
  • Review SMS retention policies for sensitive client data
  • Monitor for phishing attempts targeting exposed clients
