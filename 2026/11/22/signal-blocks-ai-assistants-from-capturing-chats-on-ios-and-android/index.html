Signal blocks AI assistants from capturing chats on iOS and Android

Signal has introduced protections to prevent Siri and Gemini from accessing private conversations, citing the risk of sensitive data leaving the app's control.

Signal announced new measures to block screenshots on iOS 27 and prevent AI assistants from capturing conversations on supported Android devices. The company stated that these protections stop OS-level features, such as Siri's Onscreen Awareness and Google's Gemini Intelligence, from extracting chat content displayed on the screen.

Meredith Whittaker, Signal's president, described the ability of AI agents to access conversation data as an "existential threat" to the service. She noted that because these agents are embedded deep in operating systems, apps have limited options for protecting users, and current protections may not cover all extraction pathways.

Signal recommends users upgrade to the latest available version and verify that "Block Siri AI" is enabled in the app's security settings on iOS. For macOS 27 users, where screenshot-blocking protection is currently unavailable, Signal advises adding the app to the Excluded Apps list in System Settings to prevent proactive access by Siri.

Affected

iOS 27, Android versions with detectable AI assistants, macOS 27

Actions

  • Update Signal to the latest available version.
  • On iOS, check Settings > App Security to ensure Block Siri AI is enabled.
  • On macOS 27, add Signal to the Excluded Apps list under System Settings > Siri.
  • On Android, ensure Screen Security is automatically enabled when AI access is detected.
