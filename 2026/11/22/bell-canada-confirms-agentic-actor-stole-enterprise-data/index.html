Bell Canada confirms 'agentic' actor stole enterprise data

Bell Canada confirmed that an 'agentic' threat actor breached its Maximo system on 17 November 2026, exfiltrating enterprise customer details including IP addresses and contact information.

Bell Canada has confirmed that an "agentic" threat actor accessed its systems on 17 November 2026, stealing enterprise customer data. The breach involved unauthorised access to a pre-production environment of the company's Maximo system, which is used to order and provision telecommunications network connections.

According to a breach notification reviewed by BleepingComputer, the data may have included business contact details such as names, job titles, corporate email addresses, and phone numbers. The dataset may also have contained ticket titles, some IP addresses associated with network elements, and limited contextual information. Bell stated that the affected data did not include credentials, highly sensitive personal information, or file attachments.

Bell said it contained the incident immediately and that there has been no impact to customer services. The company is sharing indicators of compromise (IOCs) and threat-hunting information with enterprise customers and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (CCCS), though it has not publicly released these indicators. Bell has not identified the threat actor or disclosed the number of affected customers.

Affected

Bell Canada Maximo system (pre-production environment)

Actions

  • Monitor for unsolicited requests or social engineering attempts from individuals impersonating Bell Canada.
  • Check for related activity in your environment using threat-hunting information shared by Bell Canada.
  • Review exposure of enterprise contact details and IP addresses in telecommunications provisioning systems.
