Boston Scientific breach exposes 117,789 individuals' data

A vishing attack on Boston Scientific's cloud infrastructure exposed personal and medical details for 117,789 people, marking the company's second major incident in 2026.

Boston Scientific is notifying individuals affected by a data breach that exposed personal and medical information. According to a notification sample shared with US authorities, the attacker used social engineering in a voice phishing (vishing) attack to access the company's cloud infrastructure. The unauthorised access occurred between 7 May and 1 November, with the company becoming aware of the intrusion on 12 May and confirming the breach on 5 November.

The Texas Attorney General breach portal indicates that 117,789 individuals were impacted. Exposed data included full names, physical addresses, Social Security numbers, government-issued ID numbers, medical information, health insurance details, and dates of birth. Boston Scientific stated that patient medical records and data from remote monitoring systems, connected medical devices, and clinical case management applications were not exposed.

This incident is unrelated to a previous cyberattack in August 2026 that caused network outages and operational disruptions. A spokesperson confirmed the two events are distinct. The company said it contained the incident, engaged outside cybersecurity experts, and notified law enforcement. It also stated there was no impact on manufacturing, product safety, or remote monitoring operations.

Affected

cloud-based applications and cloud-hosted data

Actions

  • Review cloud access logs for signs of social engineering or vishing attempts.
  • Verify that multi-factor authentication is enforced for all cloud infrastructure access.
  • Monitor for unauthorised access to cloud-hosted data and applications.
  • Implement strict identity verification procedures for support requests.
