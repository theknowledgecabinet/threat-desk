Independent review launched into ICO culture and governance

Barrister Karon Monaghan KC will lead an independent review of the UK data regulator following allegations of a toxic workplace culture and misconduct by its former chief.

Barrister Karon Monaghan KC has been appointed to conduct an independent review of the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), following an internal investigation into former chief John Edwards. Edwards resigned after being accused of using “vulgar and highly sexualised language” towards women, with the review expected to report in the first quarter of 2027.

The review will examine the culture, accountability, and governance of the ICO, including how complaints are raised and acted upon. It will not have powers to discipline staff or determine misconduct. The Information Commission has confirmed it will not enforce non-disclosure agreements signed by current and former staff, allowing them to speak freely to the review team.

Staff surveys conducted in May and June 2026 found that 63% of respondents considered bullying, harassment, and discrimination a serious problem, while 50% reported being victims. Unions, including PCS and FDA, have argued that the issues extend beyond the former commissioner, with 65% of survey participants identifying these behaviours as a serious concern.

Actions

  • Monitor the ICO's independent review for findings on governance and complaint handling.
  • Review internal non-disclosure agreements to ensure they do not prevent staff from reporting misconduct to independent bodies.
  • Assess workplace culture risks through anonymous staff surveys to identify systemic issues early.
