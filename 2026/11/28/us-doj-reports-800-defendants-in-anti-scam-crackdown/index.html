US DOJ reports 800 defendants in anti-scam crackdown

US prosecutors have pursued more than 400 scam cases involving over 800 defendants and disrupted 29 scam compounds following a directive to prioritise cybercrime and fraud.

The US Department of Justice (DOJ) announced on 24 November that it has pursued more than 400 scam cases involving over 800 defendants across 85 federal districts since March. The initiative follows Executive Order 14390, signed by President Donald Trump on 6 March, which directed federal agencies to prioritise cybercrime and fraud. The DOJ stated that 30 defendants have been extradited to the US, while US agents supported law enforcement in more than 10 countries during raids on compounds and call centres targeting Americans.

Under Operation Blackout, the FBI said it helped disrupt or dismantle more than 29 scam compounds across the Middle East, Africa and Asia. The agency reported restraining nearly $17bn in criminal funds and federally charging more than 50 people allegedly linked to the scams, while partner countries arrested or detained more than 320 others. The FBI also reported helping free and return more than 2,200 people trafficked into the compounds.

The US Secret Service reported restraining more than $200m and recovering over $119m, while disrupting 1,780 domains run by scammers. It has over 1,540 cases of business email compromise (BEC) under investigation, including a cryptocurrency investment fraud scheme allegedly run by scam centres in Cambodia that targeted at least 174 US victims. The State Department said it funded work that helped freeze over $1bn of stolen cryptocurrency and take down more than 1.4 million fake websites and accounts.

Actions

  • Monitor for business email compromise (BEC) indicators, as US authorities are investigating over 1,540 such cases.
  • Verify the identity of financial requesters through out-of-band communication channels to mitigate BEC risks.
  • Review third-party and vendor relationships for potential links to fraudulent investment schemes or scam compounds.
