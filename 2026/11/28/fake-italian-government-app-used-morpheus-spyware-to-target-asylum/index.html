Fake Italian government app used Morpheus spyware to target asylum seekers

An Android app mimicking the Interior Ministry used Morpheus spyware to target asylum seekers, highlighting gaps in oversight for Italy's surveillance industry.

An investigation by IrpiMedia, building on Lookout’s 2024 findings, attributes the deployment of Morpheus spyware to IPS, a Latina-based supplier to Italian prosecutors, police and intelligence agencies. The malware was distributed via an Android app called FirmaDigitale, which mimicked the website of Poste Italiane and displayed the Interior Ministry logo to promise asylum seekers news on their protection status. Police deny any link to the app, and Poste Italiane did not comment.

Osservatorio Nessuno named the spyware Morpheus in April 2026 after finding it in a fake Fastweb app. The malware can access the microphone, intercept internet calls, record the screen, track the phone, and access contacts, call logs, files and texts. It can also hijack WhatsApp through a fake biometric prompt. While the distribution site firmadigitale.assistenza-mobile[.]it has been unreachable since 2025, the app may still circulate elsewhere.

The incident underscores the expansion of Italy's surveillance sector, where spyware targets rose from 2,894 in 2021 to 5,611 in 2025. Nine established firms reported €214.4 million in 2024 revenue from surveillance tools, with Italy accounting for 72.8% of revenue among those disclosing geography. Critics note that Italian law permits spyware use beyond mafia and terrorism cases, and that there is no obligation to notify individuals if they have been subjected to surveillance once an investigation is closed.

Affected

Android devices; Morpheus spyware; FirmaDigitale app

Actions

  • Verify the source of any government-related app before installation, checking for official domains and publisher identities.
  • Monitor for unexpected battery drain or data usage, which may indicate spyware activity.
  • Review app permissions, particularly for microphone, screen recording, and contact access, and revoke unnecessary grants.
  • Report suspicious apps to relevant authorities and security vendors to aid in takedown efforts.
