About Threat Desk

Pipeline

Collected from CISA (advisories, KEV), NCSC, vendor PSIRTs and named press sources twice a day. A small model shortlists; one writer call per story works from that story's sources only; a separate fact-check pass (plus a figures/CVE/version string check) verifies every sentence; one fix attempt, else the story is dropped. CVSS and KEV status come from NVD and CISA records.

Editorial rules

Threat-actor claims are attributed as claims. Attribution only as firm as the source. No leak-site links, no exploit code. Times are UTC.
